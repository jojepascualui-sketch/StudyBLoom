StudyBloom design reference

The visual source of truth for the app. Match these colors, fonts, spacing and screens when building the Java/TeaVM UI. Every screen is drawn at 360 × 740, the smallest phone the app must support.

Shape and rules

  • Radius: cards 20px, sheets 28px, inputs 16px, buttons and tags fully round.
  • Shadow: 0 6px 18px rgba(190,24,93,0.10), pink tinted, never grey.
  • Spacing: 18px screen padding, 10px between task cards, 14px between fields.
  • Tap targets at least 44px. Visible pink focus ring on every control.
  • The bloom meter is the one signature element. Keep everything else calm.
  • Respect reduced motion: skip the petal burst, keep the toast.
